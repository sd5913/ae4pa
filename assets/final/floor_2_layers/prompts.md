1. **wall_tile / precise-object-edit**：ONE seamless horizontal modular wall texture tile ONLY. Faithfully reconstruct exposed crimson diamond damask wallpaper, dark top cornice and reddish wooden wainscot. Orthographic front view, same painted game palette. Remove doors, furniture, lamps, pictures, stairs, shadows, ropes, city and letters. Complete hidden wall. Edge-to-edge opaque; small repeated section, not an entire floor shrunk.
2. **furniture_layer / background-extraction**：Six separate alpha cutouts in 3×2 cells: top-hat portrait, tall moon/castle painting, wide castle landscape, tall leafy plant in gold urn, maroon tufted bench, pale-yellow flowers on golden pedestal. Exact source designs and colors, full silhouettes, transparent gutters; no walls, floor, doors, people or extra items. Minimally reconstruct hidden edges.
3. **doors_layer / background-extraction**：Five complete door assemblies, equal scale, 3×2 cells: 201/202/203 closed with normal bulbs; 204 closed with red bulbs and separate top alarms removed; 205 partly open with gold interior. Exact readable room plates, whole crest/bulbs/base inside cells, no floor or wall or furniture. Sixth cell empty. Minimal reconstruction, not generic door substitutions.
4. **interaction_layer / background-extraction**：Six cells: left stair portal excluding foreground handrail, right stair portal excluding handrail, gold 2F sign; package cabinet with drawers/pigeonholes, mop and yellow bucket, large sealed envelope. Same red/gold/purple game design. Reconstruct occluded stair/cabinet edges, true transparent backgrounds; no halos, people, wall or floor.
5. **foreground_layer / background-extraction**：Six cells: left black exterior column without sign/city, right exterior column without city, three gold posts and red velvet ropes; separate left diagonal stair handrail, separate right handrail, standalone hexagonal black/gold wall lantern. Hard clean silhouette, alpha outside, no floor, city or people.
6. **fx_layer / lighting extraction**：Six separate effects: soft gold lamp glow; red warning glow; paired red alarm sirens with small bases; orange angular alarm rays; golden door spill trapezoid; subtle neutral contact shadow. Transparent alpha falloff, no wall/floor/door/character; only siren cell contains physical lamp objects.
7. **floor_tile / precise-object-edit**：Only one small horizontal repeatable side-view floor/carpet module matching crimson walking band, thin gold edge and dark structural fascia. Orthographic sidescroller, not top-down. No wall/wainscot/furniture/stairs/doors/people. Native crop excludes top/bottom generated padding; no resampling.
8. **skyline_tile / precise-object-edit**：Only modular distant outside-night panel based on purple city slivers at source edges. Sparse dark gothic roof silhouettes along lower third, muted violet sky above. No interior, window frame, objects, signs, people, extra moon or stars. Opaque edge-to-edge backdrop reconstructed from visible source fragments.
